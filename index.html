Interest form to live course

From application to your first cohort in 14 days

Every facilitator goes through the same path. You are never doing any of it alone.

DAY 01

Submit the interest form

Ten minutes. Tell us who you are, what you want to teach, and who you want to teach it to.

DAY 02 – 03

We review your application

Our team checks your experience and how your topic fits what our learners are asking for. You hear back either way.

DAY 04

Discovery call

A 30 minute call to understand your expertise, your availability, and the learners you want to reach.

DAY 05 – 06

Agree how we work together

We confirm your course scope, delivery format, teaching schedule, and how you get paid. You sign a facilitator agreement.

DAY 07

Your first virtual training workshop

A live session with our team on the Rubikcon teaching method, the platform tools, and how to structure a lesson that holds attention.

DAY 08 – 10

Build your curriculum

You draft your modules, lessons, and assessments using what you learnt in the workshop. Our team works with you through it.

DAY 11 – 12

Quality review

We review your curriculum against the Rubikcon standard for clarity, practical value, and learner outcomes, then send notes for you to apply.

DAY 13

Upload and go live

Your course goes onto the platform with your facilitator profile, and we open enrolment.

DAY 14 →

Build your community

We help you set up your learner community, bring your first cohort in, and keep supporting you as it grows. This part does not end on day 14.

Before you start

What the interest form asks

Have these ready and the form takes about ten minutes.

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone or WhatsApp number
  • Country and city
  • LinkedIn profile or portfolio link
  • Current role and organisation
  • Years of experience in your field
  • Area of expertise, for example blockchain, AI, product, data, design, cybersecurity
  • Proposed course title and a one line description
  • Who the course is for and what they will be able to do after it
  • Any teaching, training, or speaking experience
  • Preferred format: live cohort, self paced, or both
  • Hours you can commit each week
  • Languages you can teach in
  • Whether you already have a community or audience, and its size
  • A two minute intro video link (optional but it helps)
  • How you heard about Rubikcon
